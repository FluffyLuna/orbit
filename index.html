Orbit

A personal organizer for the desktop

Orbit keeps notes, tasks, goals, a calendar and email in one place, with reminders, habits and a focus timer.

What it does

  • Notes and tasks, with due dates, repeats and reminders.
  • A calendar with events and reminders.
  • Goals and habits, with streaks that forgive a missed day.
  • Email: connect a Gmail account to read, search, write and organize your email inside Orbit, and turn an email into a task, note or calendar event.
Orbit is a private project made for one person. It runs only on its owner's own computer and isn't available to download.
